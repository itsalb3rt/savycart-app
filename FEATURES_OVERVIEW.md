# SavyCart Features Overview

## Mobile Application Features

### Product Management
- Product listing with search and filtering
- Add/edit/view/delete products
- Favorites functionality
- Product categories and measurement units
- Product brands management

### Shopping Cart System
- Add products to shopping cart
- Quantity management
- Shopping cart persistence
- Purchase history tracking

### Shopping Process
- Shopping registration with establishment name
- Purchase creation and saving
- Tax calculations
- Offline support

### Analytics & History
- Purchase history tracking
- Shopping history with details view
- Financial summaries

### Miscellaneous Features
- Settings management
- User profile management
- Measurement units management
- Categories management
- Currency support (multi-currency)
